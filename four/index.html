You win

Tap a column (or arrows and Enter) to drop your next disc. Win with four of yours in a row, or four in a row of any colours that make ten, two of them yours. Discs are mostly 1s. On Easy and Medium a blue ring marks where the computer's next disc would win; on Hard it can also Pop, Hold and Tune. Block a three or set up a ten to earn power-ups.
