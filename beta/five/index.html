How to play

Tap your tile, then any tile to add it onto (or arrows, Enter). A tile at 5 or more bursts. A round is yours when both computer tiles have burst (whoever burst them); first to 3 wins. Bursting theirs earns power-ups. The computer goes first; you can always win.

Keys: arrows move, Enter picks, 1 to 4 (or U for Undo) use power-ups, Esc cancels.
