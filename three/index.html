How to play

Swipe or use the arrow keys: every tile moves one step. 1 and 2 make 3; from 3 up equal tiles merge, and so do 3 and 6 (they make 9). Line up 3, 6, 9 in a row or column to flush it away for points and a power-up.

  • 1 and 2 (the small grey halves) make 3. Two 1s or two 2s never merge.
  • From 3 up, equal tiles merge.
  • 3 and 6 make 9 too. 1 + 2 and 3 + 6 are the only unequal pairs.
  • Flush: line up 3, 6, 9 (or 6, 9, 12) in a row or column for big points and a power-up. A flush uses only 3, 6, 9 and 12.
  • Big tiles grow by doubling (12, 24, 48 and 9, 18, 36). Every value has its own colour; past 384 they repeat with a ring.
  • A flush that a power-up lines up scores but earns no power-up, and neither does the swipe right after Smash, Swap or Third.

Keys: arrows move. 1 to 4 (or U for Undo) use the power-ups. Arrows and Enter aim them; Esc cancels.
