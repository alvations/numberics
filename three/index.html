How Three works

Swipe or use the arrow keys: every tile moves one step.

  • += 1 and 2 (the small grey halves) make 3.
  • += Equal tiles merge.
  • += Next-door multiples of 3 merge too: 6 and 9 make 15.
  • → Flush: 3 or more in a line, counting by 3, collapse in a rainbow for big points and a power-up.
  • Past 21 the colours start again with a ring, one more each lap. Every tile is 3 times something, so 123 is normal.
