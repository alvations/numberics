Tap a tile, then a pip or + and - to choose how much to take; a tile at 0 is gone. Keys: arrows, Enter, 1-4, L for Learn. Take the last tile to win the round; best of five wins the match and adds a tile. Easy and Medium always deal you a winning start, so perfect play wins; Hard deals at random, so a bad start needs Pass or Add. Win a match to unlock Misere, where the last take loses.
