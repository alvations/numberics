How to play

Tap a tile, then a pip or + and - to set the take (keys: arrows, Enter, 1-4, L). Win a best of five to add a tile; lose a match and the run starts again. A won round earns a power-up, unless you used Add, Pass or Peek in it. Easy and Medium deal you a winning start. Hard deals at random, so even perfect play can lose. On Hard the computer can also Pass and Add. A match win unlocks Misère (last take loses).
