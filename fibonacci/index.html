How to play

Slide with arrow keys, WASD or a swipe. Neighbours in the sequence merge: 1+1, 1+2, 2+3, 3+5 and on. Equal tiles do not merge, except 1 + 1. Every new highest tile from 55 up earns a power-up. Swapped and boosted tiles never earn a reward, and neither does the move right after a Smash, Swap or Boost. Pass 2584 and keep going.

Keys: arrows or WASD slide. 1 to 4 pick a power-up, and U is Undo. Arrows and Enter aim it. Esc cancels.
