Tap your tile, then any tile to add its value onto it (or arrows, Enter). A tile at 5 or more bursts. Burst both computer tiles to win the round; first to 3 rounds wins (the dots count them). The computer goes first, and you can always win with the right moves. Bursting a computer tile earns a power-up.
