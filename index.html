How to play

Slide with arrow keys, WASD or a swipe. Equal tiles merge. Every new highest tile from 32 up earns a power-up. Swapped and boosted tiles never earn a reward, and neither does the move right after a Smash, Swap or Boost. Keys 1 to 4 use the power-ups. Merge past 2048 and keep going.
